I designed explanations and exercises that linked underlying principles with implementation, problem solving, and interpretation of results. I also assessed student learning and advised technical projects. Earlier, at the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, I supported communications coursework and laboratory experiments, including assignments, technical materials, and grading. At COMSATS University Islamabad, I mentored students working on engineering research projects and theses.

These experiences align particularly well with teaching opportunities in computer applications and programming, communications, data networking, and signal-processing-related subjects. I can use Python, C/C++, MATLAB, simulation, and measured data to help students see how mathematical and computing concepts translate into observable system behavior. My work with AT&T Labs, Futurewei, and AT&T has further strengthened my ability to explain wireless networks, network measurements, technical troubleshooting, and the practical constraints faced by engineering teams. I would adapt laboratories and assignments to departmental learning outcomes, available equipment, and the preparation of the students enrolled.

I value inclusive, student-centered instruction that sets clear expectations and gives students repeated opportunities to practice, troubleshoot, receive feedback, and improve. I would be pleased to support project-based learning, departmental service, and the College's commitment to accessible public higher education. I am particularly drawn to Farmingdale's applied approach and its Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology and Telecommunications offerings.

Thank you for considering my application for the adjunct faculty pool. I would welcome an opportunity to discuss how my teaching and engineering background could meet the department's instructional needs as openings arise.
