**Re: Lecturer / Assistant Professor in Computer Science — Artificial Intelligence (January 2027)**

Dear Members of the Recruitment Committee:

I am applying for the Computer Science faculty position at The British University in Dubai, with a primary specialisation in **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** and particular interest in appointment at the Assistant Professor level. I earned my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Oklahoma in 2025, where my dissertation focused on AI/ML for wireless networks. My background combines peer-reviewed research in robust machine learning, reinforcement learning, generative and transfer learning, and intelligent sensing; university-level teaching and student mentoring; and applied AI development at AT&T, AT&T Labs, and Futurewei.

My scholarship centres on a question that extends across computer science applications: how can intelligent systems learn reliably from limited or changing data and make dependable decisions in real environments? In peer-reviewed journal and conference work, I have investigated data-efficient propagation models, generative augmentation, AI-based network failure detection, and reinforcement-learning-driven recovery. More recently, I have applied deep learning to smartwatch sensor streams for medication-adherence monitoring, extending these interests into time-series learning and digital health. My publications include articles in *Computer Communications*, *Sensors*, and *IEEE Network*, as well as IEEE conference venues. In industrial work, I have developed and evaluated AI-enabled diagnostic workflows and applied ML systems for operational telecommunications, strengthening my interest in reproducibility, model robustness, and real-world evaluation.

At the University of Lahore, I taught undergraduate **Computer Programming, Communication Systems, and Wireless Communication**. At the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, I supported communications laboratories and student assessment, and at COMSATS University Islamabad I mentored Bachelor's and Master's student research projects and thesis-related work. At BUiD, I could contribute to undergraduate and postgraduate teaching in programming, machine learning, deep learning, AI applications, data analytics, and research methods. I would develop project-based assignments in which students establish baselines, implement models in Python, evaluate generalisation, and communicate limitations. My experience with multicultural academic and engineering teams would support a collaborative learning environment.

I am particularly interested in contributing to BUiD's BSc Computer Science–AI concentration, MSc Artificial Intelligence, and PhD Computer Science programmes. My proposed research agenda would develop **trustworthy and data-efficient AI** across intelligent networks, sensing, and responsible autonomous decision-making, with opportunities for MSc dissertations, undergraduate capstones, interdisciplinary doctoral collaboration, and UAE industry engagement. I would pursue external and collaborative research funding while contributing to curriculum development, accreditation-oriented assessment, and professional workshops.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the university's research-led teaching and its mission of internationally relevant scholarship that responds to regional priorities. Thank you for considering my application. I have enclosed my CV and teaching and research statements, and I would be pleased to discuss how my AI research and engineering experience could support the Faculty's programmes.
