**Re: Adjunct Instructor, Electrical and Computer Engineering — RQ29408 (EE 583 Wireless Communications)**

Dear Members of the Search Committee:

I am writing to apply for the Adjunct Instructor opportunity in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology, with a particular interest in **EE 583 Wireless Communications**. I earned my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Oklahoma in 2025, following master's and bachelor's degrees in Telecommunication Engineering. My background combines university teaching, peer-reviewed research on AI-enabled wireless networks, and engineering experience with AT&T Labs, Futurewei, and AT&T. I would welcome the opportunity to draw on that combination of theoretical and practical experience in Stevens' graduate classrooms.

My teaching experience includes serving as a Lecturer at the University of Lahore, where I taught **Wireless Communication, Communication Systems, and Computer Programming**. I prepared course materials and assessments, guided technical discussions, and supported student projects. Earlier, at the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, I contributed to communications laboratory instruction and experiments; at COMSATS University Islamabad, I mentored students engaged in engineering research and simulation. These appointments helped shape an approach to teaching in which students develop strong analytical foundations and then test their understanding through computation, measurements, and purposeful engineering problems.

My doctoral research examined how machine learning can improve wireless-system reliability when measurements are limited or real-world conditions differ from training data. I worked on **propagation modeling and digital twins, domain-informed generative data augmentation, intelligent outage detection, and self-healing network optimization**, contributing to publications in journals and IEEE conferences. At AT&T Labs and Futurewei, I gained additional experience with wireless network measurements, LTE/5G performance analysis, and data-driven engineering. My current role at AT&T also involves applying machine learning to telecommunications diagnostics and operational workflows. These perspectives would help me relate channel models, signal quality, interference, modulation, and network performance to real systems and engineering tradeoffs.

I also make a deliberate effort to keep my technical knowledge current. Through Coursera, I completed **4G Network Fundamentals**, **Business Considerations for 5G with Edge, IoT, and AI**, and additional telecommunications AI and network-optimization coursework. I am continuing my study of **5G Network Fundamentals**. Alongside my professional and research activities, this continuing education helps me connect established wireless principles with the evolution of cellular architectures, edge intelligence, and emerging 6G research questions.

At Stevens, I would emphasize clear derivations, worked problems, Python/MATLAB simulations, and thoughtfully designed projects that require students to compare theory with measured or simulated evidence. My immediate teaching strengths are **wireless communications, communication systems, signals and systems, signal processing, and networked systems**. I would be pleased to contribute to rigorous, inclusive graduate instruction that strengthens both conceptual understanding and practical judgment.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of contributing to the Stevens ECE community.
