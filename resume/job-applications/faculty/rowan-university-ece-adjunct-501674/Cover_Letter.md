I am applying for the Adjunct Faculty position in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rowan University. I earned my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Oklahoma in 2025, focusing on artificial intelligence and machine learning for reliable and resilient wireless networks. I bring a combination of university-level teaching, research and laboratory mentoring, and practical engineering experience at AT&T, AT&T Labs, Futurewei, and Impac Exploration Services. Rowan's emphasis on experiential learning and engineering clinics particularly appeals to me: I aim to teach technical principles through analysis, experiments, and implementation rather than lectures alone.

My strongest teaching contributions would be in **communication systems and wireless communications, signals and systems, digital signal processing, digital image processing, and machine learning/artificial intelligence**. These areas align with my coursework, doctoral research, applied software and signal-analysis experience, and telecommunications industry work. I would be especially interested in **ECE 09433 Electrical Communication Systems, ECE 09432 Wireless Communications, ECE 09341 Signals and Systems, ECE 09351 Digital Signal Processing, ECE 09452 Introduction to Digital Image Processing, ECE 09455 Machine Learning, and ECE 09454 Introduction to Modern Neural Networks**, subject to departmental scheduling and course needs.

Previously, as a Lecturer in the Department of Technology at the University of Lahore, I taught undergraduate **Computer Programming, Communication Systems, and Wireless Communication**, developed course exercises and assessment materials, and guided student projects. At the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, I supported communications teaching and laboratory activities. At COMSATS University Islamabad and the University of Oklahoma, I mentored undergraduate and graduate students in simulations, research methodology, technical writing, and reproducible experiments. These experiences taught me the value of clear expectations, feedback, inclusive participation, and projects that build from fundamentals toward independent problem-solving.

My doctoral work involved data-efficient learning, wireless propagation modeling, cellular-network simulation, and AI-enabled network resilience. I also participated in research on wearable-sensor signal processing and intelligent activity recognition, which can provide practical exercises in time-series analysis, feature extraction, classification, and model evaluation. In my current AT&T machine-learning engineering work, I apply Python-based analytics and classification to telecommunications network diagnostics; one root-cause classification workflow improved automated classification performance from approximately 20% to 80%. Prior research at AT&T Labs and Futurewei, and cloud data engineering at Impac, inform my use of authentic engineering constraints, version control, validation, and careful interpretation of results.

For Rowan's project-based curriculum, I envision assignments in which students compare simulated modulation and channel behavior, implement digital filters and analyze frequency responses, evaluate image restoration or recognition methods, and build transparent neural-network baselines for engineering signals. These are prospective teaching examples, not representations that I have previously taught each named Rowan course. I would also be open to **introductory digital systems, classical control systems, and RF/microwave-related courses** where there is a departmental need, following a review of the syllabus, equipment, and required laboratory methods. My formal preparation in those subjects dates primarily to my earlier engineering degrees; I would refresh the relevant material before accepting such an assignment.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Rowan's ECE and Electrical Engineering Technology programs and collaborate with faculty on meaningful hands-on learning. Enclosed are my current CV, teaching interests and philosophy statement, and reference information; transcripts can be provided through the application portal. Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to discussing how my teaching and engineering background can support your students.
