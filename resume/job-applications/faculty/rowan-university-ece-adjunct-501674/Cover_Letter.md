I would be particularly interested in **ECE 09433 Electrical Communication Systems, ECE 09432 Wireless Communications, ECE 09341 Signals and Systems, ECE 09351 Digital Signal Processing, ECE 09452 Introduction to Digital Image Processing, ECE 09455 Machine Learning, and ECE 09454 Introduction to Modern Neural Networks**. The enclosed teaching statement explains my preparation and course preferences in greater detail.

I developed exercises and assessments, guided student projects, and emphasized conceptual understanding through practical examples. At UET Taxila, I supported communications laboratories; at COMSATS University Islamabad and the University of Oklahoma, I mentored undergraduate and graduate researchers in simulation, analysis, experimental design, and technical communication.

My doctoral research investigated wireless-network simulation, propagation modeling, data-efficient learning, and resilient communication systems. Related research in wearable sensing and signal analysis provides additional examples for DSP, image-processing and machine-learning applications. Currently, as a Machine Learning Engineer at AT&T, I develop data-driven telecommunications diagnostic methods, including a root-cause classification workflow that improved automated performance from approximately 20% to 80%. Prior industry work has also strengthened my experience with Python, practical software workflows, and rigorous validation. I would use nonproprietary examples and reproducible coding exercises to help students understand both engineering principles and implementation tradeoffs.

I am also open to **introductory digital systems, control systems, and RF/microwave-related courses** following appropriate refresher study, a review of course expectations, and preparation for any specialized laboratory equipment. I distinguish that willingness to prepare from areas in which I have already taught or practiced extensively.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Rowan's ECE and Electrical Engineering Technology programs and to mentor students through meaningful projects. Thank you for considering my application. I would be pleased to discuss how my academic and industry background could support your department.
