Kettering's curriculum offers particularly strong opportunities to contribute to **EE-430 Communication Systems, EE-340 Electromagnetic Wave Propagation, EE-336/338 Signals and Systems, and the signal-processing aspects of EE-434 Digital Signal Processing**, as well as computational engineering and student design projects. My expertise in Python, MATLAB, wireless measurements, signal and data analysis, and simulation can support meaningful hands-on learning. For additional foundational electrical-engineering and laboratory assignments, I would align my preparation with course objectives, equipment, safety requirements, and established departmental practices.

My research investigates reliable, data-efficient AI for wireless and networked systems, including domain-informed generative models, transfer learning, outage management, and learning-based optimization. I have published in *Computer Communications*, *Sensors*, *IEEE Network*, and IEEE conferences. My industry work extends this scholarship to operational diagnostics and large-scale network data, providing authentic problems for student research and applied engineering projects. At Kettering, I would develop a focused teacher-scholar program linking intelligent sensing, communications, and resilient connected systems, with undergraduate participation and industry-informed collaboration.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Kettering's undergraduate and graduate education, applied scholarship, and academic community. Thank you for considering my application.
