I am applying for Villanova University's continuing non-tenure-track Assistant Teaching Professor position in Electrical Engineering, beginning in Spring 2027. My preparation combines undergraduate engineering technology teaching, communications laboratory instruction, student advising and mentoring, and applied engineering research and development at AT&T Labs, Futurewei, and AT&T. I am attracted to a full-time teaching role centered on strong foundations, accessible mentoring, and meaningful laboratory and design experiences.

As a Lecturer in the Department of Technology at the University of Lahore, I taught **Computer Programming, Communication Systems, and Wireless Communication** to undergraduate Electrical Engineering Technology students. I prepared instructional materials, evaluated assignments and examinations, guided student projects, and helped students connect mathematical principles to experiments and engineering practice. Earlier, at the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, I supported communications laboratories and coursework. At COMSATS University Islamabad, I mentored undergraduate and graduate students on research projects, simulation, and technical reporting. These experiences have shaped my emphasis on clear explanations, practical exercises, and timely feedback.

My strongest established teaching areas are programming, communications, signals, simulation, and data-driven engineering. I recognize Villanova's particular interest in **circuits and control systems**. My ECE training and experience with system modeling, dynamic optimization, signal analysis, and engineering computation provide relevant preparation for developing instruction in those areas. To prepare for assigned circuits and control systems courses, I would use the department's learning objectives, laboratory resources, and faculty expertise to develop rigorous, well-scaffolded instruction. For example, students can connect circuit transients or feedback-response predictions with MATLAB/Python simulations and measured results, explain discrepancies, and practice systematic troubleshooting.

I would also value the opportunity to advise students on course pathways, senior design, independent projects, and professional development. My industry background helps me connect classroom decisions with engineering reliability, documentation, teamwork, and responsible use of computational tools. In teaching and advising, I aim to create a learning environment that welcomes different starting points while holding all students to clear technical standards. Villanova's Augustinian commitments to truth, unity, and love resonate with an approach that treats intellectual honesty, collaboration, and care for students as inseparable from engineering excellence.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Villanova's ECE curriculum, laboratories, capstone mentoring, and departmental community.
