\section{Research Trajectory for Optimization for Machine Learning}
My forward research program centers on optimization and learning for reliable networked intelligent systems: (1) robust and adaptive optimization for ML under distribution shift, uncertainty, and limited labels; (2) sequential and hierarchical decision-making for autonomous network control and AI-enabled systems; (3) distributed and communication-efficient learning and optimization for wireless/edge environments; and (4) trustworthy AI optimization emphasizing reliability, robustness, interpretability, and rigorous evaluation. This agenda builds directly on my prior work in multi-parameter network optimization, reinforcement learning, domain adaptation, digital twins, outage management, and experimentally validated wireless AI.
